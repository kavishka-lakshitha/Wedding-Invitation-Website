Good to know

Questions & Answers

A few little details to help you plan your day with us.

Can I bring a plus one?
We would love to welcome your plus one. Please include their name in your RSVP so we can save them a seat and a plate.
What should I wear?
Elegant wedding attire is perfect — sarees, lehengas, gowns, suits or national dress. Please avoid white and ivory, which are reserved for the bride.
Is there parking at the venue?
Yes. Complimentary valet parking is available at The Kingsbury. We recommend arriving by 8:45 AM so you can settle in before the ceremony.
Are children welcome?
Absolutely. Your little ones are welcome to celebrate with us — please include them in your guest count so we can plan accordingly.
Will there be a gift registry?
Your presence is the greatest gift. If you wish to honour us with something more, a contribution towards our new home would be received with heartfelt gratitude.
How do I get to The Kingsbury?
The Kingsbury is at 48 Janadhipathi Mawatha, Colombo 01. Use the map in the Venue section or tap “Get Directions” for turn-by-turn navigation.
Can I take photos during the ceremony?
We would love you to be fully present with us during the Poruwa ceremony. Our photographer will capture every moment — feel free to take photos during the reception and celebrations.
